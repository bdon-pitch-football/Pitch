# 36 · Note for John — free at launch, and what the Terms still say

> **Doc 36 · v1.0.** Written by Leo for John. **Nothing in this note is a
> decision.** Each item gives the facts, the question, and what happens if you
> say nothing.

## What changed

On 28 Sep BUZ decided that **Pitch is free at launch, the Interest Register
included** (register D-163). No card is taken anywhere, and Premium is priced
later. The Stripe build stays in the code, switched off. **Nothing about
verification changes:** a club reads the register only after a human has
verified it (D-126).

## 1 · Schedule A still sells the register

Doc 22 v2.0 Schedule A is written as a paid contract:
- A3 fees and term
- A4 renewal notices
- A5.1 the 14-day cooling-off on $329
- A6 and A6.1 prices ($54 / $329, "a saving of $319")
- A6.2 tax invoices
- 8.6 paid subscriptions

None of this can happen at launch.

**The same schedule also carries the obligations a free register still needs:**
- A1.1, whether the person was authorised
- A8(e), use only for the club's own trials, no disclosure, no taking or keeping
- A8.3, no contacting a player directly, ever
- A10, the family never pays
- A11, no export of the register

**Question:** is Schedule A reshaped for a free register? For example: keep
A1, A2, A8, A10 and A11, and take out the fee, renewal, cooling-off and tax
invoice clauses until a price exists. This would follow your ruling of this
morning, that clauses describing what the product does not do are removed
until it does. And does it land as a new version, with club acceptance
re-asked when pricing returns?

**If you say nothing:** the Terms keep describing a paid product that nobody
can buy.

## 2 · The live website serves an old copy of the Terms

`pitchfootball.com.au/terms` is served from the website's own branch, not from
the app. It shows **doc 22 v1.7, with the whole drafting preamble, the
`[DRAFTED]` markers and the prices**: 14 price mentions as of 28 Sep. That is
exactly what you ruled on this morning (item 1: stripping the preamble is not
material; the version still bumps). The fix exists only in the app.

**Question:** may the website serve the same stripped, current text as the
app? That is doc 22 v2.0 (or whatever item 1 above produces), and the same for
`/privacy`.

**If you say nothing:** the website keeps showing a parent our drafting notes
and a price we no longer charge.

## 3 · Two smaller ones

- **After a child is deleted, do we keep the anonymous record of who looked at
  their record and why?** The deletion fix (D-26) is being built to keep it,
  with no link back to the child. If the answer is no, it is a small change.
- **Suppression (22 §6.5).** It was built on 17 Sep, and nobody has yet
  confirmed it works end to end. That box is still unticked on the launch gate.
