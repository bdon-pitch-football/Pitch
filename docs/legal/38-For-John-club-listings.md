# 38 · John: listing Victorian clubs we have not spoken to, and their trials

> **Doc 38 · v1.0.** From Leo (head of technology), at BUZ's request, 30 September 2026, the evening Pitch went live.
> **BUZ's question:** "Are we legally allowed to advertise and put up clubs' trials on our page with these profiles, all based off data we find online for these clubs?"
> **Nothing is loaded until you answer.** Each item has a recommendation you can answer with "agree" or change.

## Why this has come up tonight

- **The problem:** Pitch went live around 6pm. Clubs could not sign up. The claim flow needs the club to already have a page, and production started with none.
- **What we fixed, with approved words:**
  - a **Find your club** search;
  - a **Tell us your club** request that BUZ adds by hand after checking the address.
- **What is ready but held for you:** a list to load of about **180 Victorian clubs** as *unclaimed* listings (D-64). That is the whole NPL, VPL and State League 1–7 pyramid.

## What the list is, and where it came from

- **Size:** 209 clubs; 182 are loadable tonight (the rest have no suburb yet). 138 of those 182 have an email.
- **Where the clubs came from:** Football Victoria's published 2026 league structures, confirmed against FV's news and ladder reports.
- **Four clubs removed:**
  - Western United and Mazenod, which withdrew from 2026 competitions;
  - Melbourne City and Melbourne Victory academies, which are professional clubs rather than community ones.
- **Per club we hold:**
  - name, suburb, state and tier;
  - the club's **own published** general, secretary or enquiries email, taken from the club's own website on 30 Sep;
  - the exact page it came from.
- **Emails left out:**
  - any address that names a person;
  - sponsor or vendor addresses;
  - addresses from council directories;
  - "leads" we could not confirm on a club page. Those clubs have no email, so they can only claim after BUZ calls them.
- **Nobody was contacted.**

## What an unclaimed club page shows

- **Content:** the club's name, suburb and state.
- **Banner:** **"Compiled from public information — not affiliated until claimed"** (D-64).
- **Email the club:** a mailto link to the club's own published address.
- **What it does not show:** no crest, logo, photos, people, players or children, and no register.
- **Search engines:** kept out (`noindex`, and not in the sitemap) until the club claims.
- **Removal:** **Report a page** is in the footer. BUZ can remove a listing from ops in one click.
- **Claiming:** the code goes only to the club's published address. Access to anything involving players waits for BUZ's verification call (D-126).

## Trials on those pages (D-90, as built)

- **Who can post:** a verified club posts its own notices. For any other club, **Pitch may compile a notice from the club's own public notice**.
- **What a compiled notice carries:**
  - a required link to that source;
  - "added on" and "last checked" stamps;
  - expiry after its date;
  - the unclaimed disclaimer.
- **What families see:** that the notice is compiled, not posted by the club.
- **Public submissions:** there is no public submission route (D-90).

## The questions

1. **Can we load the list?** Loading means about 180 real clubs as unclaimed listings: facts only, with the banner above, noindexed, and removed on request.
   - *Recommendation:* **yes**. This is the D-64 design at scale.
   - *If we hear nothing:* the list stays unloaded. Clubs use "Tell us your club".
2. **Is the banner wording enough?** It reads "Compiled from public information — not affiliated until claimed". The concern is Australian Consumer Law ss 18 and 29(1)(h) (implied sponsorship or affiliation).
   - *Recommendation:* keep it, and show it at the top of the page, which it is.
   - Say if you want stronger wording, for example "Pitch Football has no connection with this club until it claims this page."
3. **Can we show "Email the club" on an unclaimed page?** The address is one the club publishes itself. It is not personal information, because role addresses only are used.
   - *Recommendation:* **yes**.
   - The alternative is to hide the link until the club claims.
4. **Can we compile trials?** A compiled trial restates the facts (dates, venue, age groups) from the club's own public notice. It never copies their poster or wording, and always links to the notice.
   - *Recommendation:* **yes**, as D-90 already allows.
5. **Can clubs be named in marketing?** This covers paid ads, social posts and emails that name an unclaimed club, for example "Green Gully trials on Pitch".
   - *Recommendation:* **no, not until the club has claimed its page or BUZ has spoken to them.** That is the line where a directory starts to look like an endorsement.
6. **Removals:** a club that asks to come off should be removed the same day, with no questions and no further contact.
   - *Recommendation:* **yes**. It is a written rule for BUZ and Leo, and needs no new words.

## What we are not asking

- **Club outreach emails.** GTM already works under the Spam Act rules for conspicuously published addresses. They are unchanged by this note.
- **Crests and logos.** None are used, and we are not proposing to use any.
