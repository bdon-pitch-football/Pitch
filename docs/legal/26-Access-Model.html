Who sees what

The whole access model, in plain English. Written for a technical director who is about to be personally accountable for what a platform does with their players' information, and would rather read one page than a policy.

The one sentence

Children are visible, and never contactable. A club can see a player. Nobody can reach a child. Everything below is that sentence, applied.

The Interest Register — what you get, and what you never get

The one thing a club can pay us for. A player registers interest — in a trial you have posted, or in your club generally — and appears in a standing list you filter and work through.

  • You receive a link, not a record. Their name, age, current club, the squad, a position, one line they wrote, and a link to a page their family controls. Not a file. Not a copy.
  • The family can switch it off. If they do, the link dies and their line disappears from your list the same minute.
  • There is no download. No spreadsheet, deliberately — a downloaded list would survive a family switching off, which would make everything above untrue.
  • You never get a phone number or an email address for a player or a family. An invitation lands inside the guardian's Pitch account. That is not held back for a higher tier; it does not exist at any price.
  • A free club is not a less safe club. Free clubs receive CVs from families the same way, with the same protections for the same children. The subscription buys organisation. It has never bought protection and it never will.

Technical director is granted by the club, never claimed by a person. It is the highest-privilege role in the product — club-wide read across children's records — and "I am the TD" typed into a form is a claim, not a credential. We confirm it when we verify your club.

A player who leaves you

The record belongs to the player, so it goes with them. Your club keeps aggregate figures. Your coaches keep what they personally wrote.

This is the part clubs ask about most, so plainly: we are not going to help you keep a player's history hostage, and we are not going to let their next club take yours. The record follows the child. That is the same rule whichever end of it you are on, and it is the reason a family will let you write in it at all.

Everyone else

Right now, nobody searches for a player at all

In the launch product discovery runs one way: players find clubs. A family sends you a CV. You post a trial and players come to it. A coach or a parent passes a link. There is no search over players, and it is not built.

Things Pitch will never do

  • No agents, ever. There is no agent role, no agent access tier, and no plan to build one. The record we hold would make a lead list, and it is not going to be one.
  • No rival club can see your players. Search reaches players aged 16 and over who have guardian has not switched it off — we notify the guardian thirty days before a player turns sixteen, and the switch is theirs. It does not reach your squad lists, your assessments or your club-wide view.
  • No rankings of children. No leaderboards, no scores out of ten, no "potential" rating. Development is measured against what is expected at an age stage, never against team-mates.
  • No selling anyone's information. Not to sponsors, not to advertisers, not to anyone. If Pitch is ever sold, the buyer is bound by the same rules and families are told first.
  • No charging a family to see their own child's record. Ever. That is the thing you get to give them.

What we ask of you

Two things, and the second is the one that gets forgotten.

  • Verifying that your coaches may work with children is your job, not ours. Pitch records what you attest and treats it as a gate, not a badge — an unverified coach at your own club sees nothing, and if your club loses verified status everyone at it loses access to every child's record immediately, including your technical director. It does not discharge your obligation, and we will never suggest that it does.
  • Do not name a child on a public page. Your club page, your alumni list, your trial notices — "a 2019 U13 now in an NPL squad" is a better line anyway, and it does not publish a searchable identity you do not have permission to publish.

If something is wrong

Every page has a report link. Anyone can use it — no account, no reason needed. We respond within one business day, and immediately where a child is at risk. A parent who wants their child taken off something does not have to explain themselves to us, and will not be asked to.
