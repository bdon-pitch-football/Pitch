## This page is for you, not your parents

There is a longer version for adults. This one is for the player.

It says what Pitch knows about you, who can see it, and what you can do about it. It is short on purpose.

---

## What we know about you

Just the football stuff, and your birthday.

- Your name
- **Your date of birth** — this is the important one. It decides who is allowed to see you and what Pitch looks like for you.
- Where you play — your club, your age group, and your city
- Your positions, your squad number, which foot you prefer
- Your photo, if you want one
- What you write about yourself
- Your stats, which **you** type in — so Pitch always shows them as "self-reported", because we did not check them
- Links to your videos. The videos stay on YouTube or Veo. We only keep the link.

## What we do not know about you

- **We do not track where you are.** No GPS. Nothing that follows you around. We know your city, because you typed it in so trials near you are useful. (When you first open the site we check which country you are in, because you can only sign up in Australia. We do not keep that.)
- **We do not know your school.** There used to be a box for it and we took it away. Your school plus your age group would tell a stranger where you are on a Tuesday morning. It is not worth it. You can add it when you turn 18.
- **We do not watch what you click.** If you are under 18, we do not follow you around the app.
- **We do not know anything about your health.** We do not ask, and there is nowhere to put it.

## Who can see your page

**If you are under 16:**

- **Your parent or guardian.** Everything you see, all the time.
- **Your own club** — your coach and the people who run it.
- **Anyone your parent sends your link to.** That is how you get seen. Your parent decides who. The link stops working after 90 days unless they renew it.
- **Nobody finds you by searching.** You are not in any search on Pitch. There is no search that can find you.

**If you are 16 or 17:**

- **Clubs cannot find you until your parent turns it on.** You start off hidden. Not hidden a bit — hidden.
- **You can ask them to turn it on.** There is a button. They press it, and then clubs and coaches we have checked can find you.
- **They can turn it off again** any time, and they do not have to tell you why. It is worth talking to them about it.
- **We start it off on purpose.** Being found is the good part of Pitch, so this is a real choice — but it is your parent's to make, not ours, and not a stranger's.

**Nobody can message you. There are no private messages on Pitch at all** — not from another player, not from a coach at another club, not from us.

**A club never gets your phone number or your parent's.** If a club wants you at a trial, the invitation turns up **inside your parent's Pitch account**. We just tell them something is waiting. We do not put your name in that message, or anything about you.

## Your parent sees everything — and now they check your changes too

If you are under 16, your parent can see everything on your account, including anything you write. We are telling you at the start because finding out later would not be fair.

**They also check your changes.** Once your page is approved, if you change your About, or a clip, or your stats, it waits for them before it goes live. You will see a "waiting on Mum" note on your own page.

**This is not because we think you would write something bad.** It is because clubs can look at your page whenever they like, and your parent said yes to the words that were there when they looked. While a change is waiting, a club still sees the last version your parent approved. Your page never goes blank.

## Sharing your page

**You can ask to share it.** There is a share button on your own CV. When you press it, the link goes to your parent to send on. It is your page and you should be able to do something with it — the sending is theirs. The button says so.

**Share cards work the same way.** If you want to post your football to Instagram or send it to a mate, you pick a card and **your parent sees the exact picture before it can go anywhere.**

**Why we are careful about the card.** Apps like WhatsApp and Instagram keep their own copy of it. We cannot get it back. Nobody can. So the card is small on purpose: **your first name, the first letter of your last name, your positions, your number and your stats. That is it.** Not your club. Not your suburb. Not your school. Not your age group.

It still looks good — it is your football. It just does not tell a stranger where to find you.

**When your link stops working**, anyone who clicks it sees a page with nothing on it. No name. No photo. No club. A club we have checked can ask your parent for a new one. Your parent does not have to answer, and the club is not told anything either way.

## Telling a club you are interested

Some clubs put their trials on Pitch. If you want to be considered:

- **You write it. Your parent presses send.** They see which club, which trial, everything that is about to go, and the line you wrote.
- **The club gets** your name, your age, your club, which squad you are interested in, your position, your line, and a link to your page. **Not a file. Not a copy.**
- **What you write in that line is read by an adult you have not met.** You do not need to put your phone number, your address or your school in it. You should not. Clubs reach you through your parent.
- **You can undo it.** If your parent switches the link off, the club loses your page *and* your line, straight away.
- **A club cannot download a list of players.** There is no spreadsheet. If your family switches off, you disappear from their list that minute.
- **No club will ever tell you no on Pitch.** There is no rejection and no status to check. If a club wants you, you and your parent hear from them together. If you do not hear, you were not invited — and that is between you and them, not something we hand you.

## Money — because you might wonder

Some clubs pay us for the list that trial interest goes into. **That changes nothing about you.**

- Your safety is not something anyone buys. It is the same whether your club pays or not.
- **You will never pay for anything on Pitch, and neither will your family.** Your record is yours and it is free, always.
- **You will never see anything on Pitch you have to pay for**, because we do not show that to anyone under 18 at all.

## Stuff you can do

- **Change your CV.** Most of it, whenever you like — your parent sees the change before it goes live.
- **Take your videos off.** Any time.
- **Ask for something to be fixed** if it is wrong.
- **Get a PDF of your CV.** Free, always.
- **Delete everything.** You can do this yourself, from Settings. So can your parent. Your page, your photo, your videos, your stats — all of it goes.

  **Two small things stay, and we want to be straight with you.** We keep a note saying your parent said yes, and later that someone said delete, with the date. That is how we prove we did what we promised. It has nothing about your football in it. And if a coach wrote about you, they keep a number — how many players they wrote about — with no names and nothing they said. Nobody can look at your football again.

- **Tell us if something is wrong.** Every page has a **Report this page** link. Use it if anything on Pitch makes you uncomfortable, or if something about you is on there that should not be. **You will not get in trouble for using it**, and you do not have to be sure. We look at reports about players first.

## About coach feedback

Later, coaches will start writing about how you are going.

- **It is never a score and never a ranking.** No marks out of ten. No list of who is best. We do not build those and we never will.
- It says what you can do and what you are working on — measured against **what is normal for your age**, never against your team-mates.
- Your coach can fix a mistake for two days. After that a correction has to be added as a new note, so nothing gets quietly changed behind your back.
- If you think something is wrong, tell your parent and they can ask us to fix it.

## Things we will never do

- **We will never sell anything about you.** Not to anyone.
- **We will never show you ads.**
- **We will never try to trick you into staying on the app.** No streaks. No "you haven't been on for a while". Nothing built to keep you scrolling — there is nothing here to scroll.
- **We will never tell anyone who looked at your page.** Not you, not your parent, not a club. When you turn 18 you can choose to see which clubs opened your CV. While you are under 18 we do not count it and nobody can buy it.

## Who runs Pitch

Pitch is run by a company. Companies have long names. Ours is **EBSD Enterprises Pty Ltd**. Its number is **ACN 701 879 718**.

You will see **Pitch** on the screen. You will see **Pitch Football** on anything about money. They are all us. Same people.

We put the long name here because it is the name a grown-up would need to write to us, or to complain about us. You should not have to go looking for it.

## Two names, and neither one is a trick

Our website is **www.pitchfootball.com.au**. Our emails come from **pitch-football.com**. They are spelled a bit differently. That is not a mistake and it is not somebody pretending to be us.

**Here is the rule that keeps you safe.** A real message from us only ever sends you to **pitchfootball.com.au**. If something says it is from Pitch and sends you somewhere else, **it is not us**. Do not tap it. Tell your parent, or email us.

## Where your information lives

On computers in **Sydney**. Not overseas. When we send an email or a text it goes through a company in America, so we keep those messages tiny — a first name and a link, never anything about your football.

When a club pays us, that goes through a payment company. **They never see anything about you.** They see a club and a card.

Email **burak.donmez@pitch-football.com**. A real person reads it. His name is Burak. You can ask what we know about you, why we have it, or to delete it.

If you would rather tell someone outside Pitch, contact the eSafety Commissioner at **esafety.gov.au**, or talk to **Kids Helpline on 1800 55 1800** — free, and open all day and all night.
