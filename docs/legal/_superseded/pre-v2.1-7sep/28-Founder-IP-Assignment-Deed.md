You made Pitch. The company now sells Pitch. Right now those are two different legal people and only one of them owns anything. **This deed moves the ownership across, for no money, permanently, and back-dates its effect to the day each thing was created.** You sign it twice — once as yourself, once as the company's sole director — with a witness present for the first signature. **THIS DEED** is made on **[date]**

## Background

- **A.** The Assignor has, since **approximately August 2026**, conceived, created, commissioned, developed and reduced to material form the concept, name, brand, designs, documents, specifications, software, text and other materials described in Schedule 1, for a player development and pathway platform for Australian football (**"Pitch"**).
- **B.** The Company was incorporated on **28 August 2026** and carries on the business of operating Pitch, trading under the registered business name *Pitch Football*.
- **C.** All of that work was done by the Assignor in his personal capacity and before the Company existed, so **no intellectual property in it vested in the Company on incorporation**.
- **D.** The Assignor wishes to assign all of that intellectual property to the Company, and the Company wishes to accept it, on the terms of this deed.

## Operative provisions

### 1. Definitions

**1.1** In this deed:

**"Assigned IP"** means all Intellectual Property Rights, anywhere in the world, in or relating to the Pitch Materials, whether existing at the date of this deed or coming into existence afterwards in respect of anything created before it.

**"Intellectual Property Rights"** means all present and future rights in copyright, designs (registered and unregistered), trade marks (registered and unregistered), patents and patentable inventions, circuit layouts, domain names, business and trading names, get-up, trade secrets, know-how and confidential information, together with all rights of action, all rights to apply for registration, and all rights to sue for past, present and future infringement.

**"Pitch Materials"** means everything described in Schedule 1 and anything else, of any kind, created by or for the Assignor in connection with Pitch before the date of this deed — **whether or not it appears in Schedule 1**, which is descriptive and not exhaustive.

**"Moral Rights"** has the meaning given in Part IX of the *Copyright Act 1968* (Cth).

### 2. Assignment

**2.1 Present assignment.** The Assignor **assigns to the Company absolutely, and free of any encumbrance, all of his right, title and interest in the Assigned IP**, including all Intellectual Property Rights in it, throughout the world, for the full term of those rights and any renewal or extension of them.

**2.2 Future copyright.** To the extent that any copyright in the Pitch Materials has not yet come into existence, the Assignor **assigns that future copyright to the Company** in accordance with **section 197 of the *Copyright Act 1968* (Cth)**, and that copyright vests in the Company immediately upon coming into existence.

**2.3 Effective from creation.** The assignment in clause 2.1 takes effect, in respect of each item of the Assigned IP, **from the moment that item was created** — not from the date of this deed. The parties intend that the Company be treated as having owned each item from its creation, and this deed is to be construed so as to give effect to that intention so far as the law permits.

**2.4 Rights of action.** The assignment includes the right to sue for, and to recover damages and other relief in respect of, **any infringement occurring before the date of this deed**, as if the Company had owned the Assigned IP at the time of that infringement.

**2.5 Nothing is held back.** The Assignor retains no right, licence, interest or claim of any kind in the Assigned IP, and **specifically retains no right to use the name "Pitch", "Pitch Football", or anything confusingly similar**, other than as a director, officer or agent of the Company.

### 3. Consideration

**3.1** This deed is **executed as a deed and is binding without consideration**. For the avoidance of doubt, the Company is not required to pay the Assignor anything for the Assigned IP, and the Assignor acknowledges that the benefit he receives as the Company's sole shareholder is sufficient reason for entering into it.

*[LEGAL] Some advisers prefer nominal consideration ($10, receipt acknowledged) even in a deed. It is harmless and it removes an argument. Counsel to say whether to add it.*

### 4. Moral rights

**4.1** The Assignor acknowledges that **Moral Rights are personal and cannot be assigned** under Australian law.

**4.2** The Assignor **genuinely consents** to the Company, and any person authorised by the Company, doing or omitting to do any act or thing that would otherwise infringe his Moral Rights in the Pitch Materials, including:

- (a) using, reproducing, adapting, altering, adding to, cutting down or destroying any of the Pitch Materials;
- (b) using any of the Pitch Materials **without attributing him as author**; and
- (c) doing anything described in (a) or (b) after the Pitch Materials have been changed by someone else.

**4.3** This consent is given **for the benefit of the Company and its successors, assigns and licensees**, extends to acts and omissions occurring before as well as after the date of this deed, and is given **freely, without duress, and not in reliance on any false or misleading statement**.

*[LEGAL] A moral rights consent must be genuine to be effective, and a consent given by a sole director to his own company is exactly the fact pattern where "genuine" gets tested. Counsel to confirm the wording and whether anything more is needed given the identity of interest.*

### 5. Registrations, names, domains and accounts

**5.1** The Assignor must, promptly and at the Company's cost, **do everything necessary to put into the Company's name** any registration, application, account or record relating to the Pitch Materials that is currently in his own name, including:

- (a) the registered business name **Pitch Football**;
- (b) the domain name **pitchfootball.com.au** and any other domain registered for Pitch;
- (c) any social media handle, account or username used or reserved for Pitch;
- (d) any account with a hosting, payment, email, messaging or software provider used for Pitch; and
- (e) any trade mark application or registration.

**5.2** Until a transfer under clause 5.1 is completed, the Assignor **holds the relevant registration, account or record on trust for the Company** and must deal with it only as the Company directs.

> **This clause is doing real work and should not be treated as boilerplate.** A registered business name held by a person other than the entity carrying on the business is a live problem under **section 18 of the *Business Names Registration Act 2011* (Cth)** — a strict liability offence, and the body corporate penalty is materially larger than the individual one. **Confirm on ASIC Connect which name holder is actually recorded**, because the register (D-148) records the business name as sitting against the company's ABN and that should be verified rather than assumed.

### 6. Delivery

**6.1** The Assignor must deliver to the Company all originals, copies, source files, working files, design files, credentials and passwords for the Pitch Materials in his possession or control, and must not retain any copy other than in his capacity as an officer of the Company.

### 7. Confidential information

**7.1** The Assignor assigns to the Company all rights in the confidential information and know-how comprised in the Pitch Materials, and must keep that information confidential except in the proper performance of his duties as an officer of the Company.

### 8. Assignor's warranties

**8.1** The Assignor warrants that:

- (a) he is the **sole owner** of the Assigned IP and has full power to assign it;
- (b) the Assigned IP is **free of any mortgage, charge, licence, option or other encumbrance**, and he has not assigned, licensed or agreed to assign or license it to anybody else;
- (c) to the best of his knowledge, **the Pitch Materials do not infringe the Intellectual Property Rights of any third party**; and
- (d) **Schedule 2 lists every person other than the Assignor who contributed to the Pitch Materials**, and is complete.

**8.2** The Assignor **indemnifies the Company** against any loss it suffers as a result of a breach of clause 8.1.

**8.3 Third-party contributions.** Where any person listed in Schedule 2 holds or may hold Intellectual Property Rights in any part of the Pitch Materials, the Assignor must **use his best endeavours to procure an assignment of those rights to the Company**, on terms no less favourable than this deed.

> ***[LEGAL] and this is the clause that can defeat the whole document.*** *Under section 35 of the *Copyright Act 1968* (Cth) the author of a work owns the copyright unless they made it as an employee — and **a freelancer, contractor or agency is not an employee**. If a designer drew the logo, a developer wrote code, or anybody was paid to produce anything, **the copyright in that thing is theirs and this deed cannot move it**, because the Assignor never owned it to assign. Each of those people needs their own assignment. Counsel to advise on the commissioning exceptions and on what to do about anyone who has since become uncontactable.*

### 9. Further assurance

**9.1** The Assignor must, at the Company's request and cost, **execute any document and do anything else reasonably necessary** to give full effect to this deed, to perfect the Company's title, or to enable the Company to register, defend or enforce the Assigned IP — including after he ceases to be a director or shareholder of the Company.

**9.2 Power of attorney.** The Assignor **irrevocably appoints the Company, and each of its directors from time to time, as his attorney** to execute any document and do anything he is required to do under clause 9.1 but has not done within 14 days of a written request. This appointment is given **for valuable consideration and to secure the Company's proprietary interest** in the Assigned IP, and is limited to that purpose.

*[LEGAL] Counsel to confirm the form of the power of attorney and whether registration is required in Victoria for it to be relied on in any dealing with land or registrable property. It is included because a founder who has left, or who is unwell, is precisely when it is needed and precisely when it cannot be obtained.*

### 10. General

**10.1 Governing law.** This deed is governed by the law of Victoria, and the parties submit to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of its courts.

**10.2 Entire agreement.** This deed records everything agreed between the parties about its subject matter.

**10.3 Variation.** Only in writing, signed by both parties.

**10.4 Counterparts and electronic execution.** This deed may be executed in counterparts and **may be signed and exchanged electronically**, and the parties agree that electronic execution is as effective as physical execution. *[LEGAL] Electronic execution of deeds is available in Victoria and for companies under section 127; counsel to confirm the position for the individual signature and whether the witness may attest by audio-visual link.*

**10.5 Severance.** If a provision is unenforceable, it is severed and the rest of the deed continues.

12. **When a trade mark is filed, file it in the Company's name.** An application in the wrong applicant's name is an ownership defect that is not always curable.
