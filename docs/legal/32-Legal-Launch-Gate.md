# 32 · The legal launch gate — what must be true before a real child's record exists

> **Doc 32 · v1.0 · 9 September 2026.** Entity: **EBSD Enterprises Pty Ltd** (ACN 701 879 718 · ABN 65 701 879 718), trading as Pitch Football.
>
> **Why this exists, and it is not tidiness.** D-47 makes **doc 14 green** the launch blocker. The PIA says **four other things are launch-blocking**. **Nobody has ever reconciled those two sentences, and they do not agree.**
>
> **Doc 14's ten conditions do not test three of the PIA's four.** I checked: `noindex` appears once in doc 14 and in no test; click-to-play, suppression, the guardian-removal path and the age-contradiction hold appear nowhere in it at all. **So doc 14 could go green tomorrow and three things the PIA calls launch-blocking would be untested.**
>
> **This document is the other half of the gate.** It does not replace doc 14 and it does not add a new opinion — **every row here is already required by a locked decision or by the PIA.** What is new is that they are in one list, phrased as something you can answer yes or no to on the morning.
>
> **The rule is D-47's rule: if any row is not green, we slip. That is a decision already made, not one to be re-argued in the moment.**

## How to use this

**Every row is a question with a yes or no answer.** Not "is it built" — **"has somebody done it and seen it work."** A control nobody has operated is a control nobody has.

**Three sections block. One does not.** Section D is named explicitly as *not* blocking so it cannot creep into the gate on the morning, and so the things that genuinely block are not diluted by the things that merely matter.

# A · The four the PIA calls launch-blocking

*These are recommendations 1, 2, 3 and 12 at §4.9. They have not changed since 25 August and nothing since has added a fifth.*

| | Must be true | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| **A1** | **A player's record can be made wholly invisible on request** — no public page, no working link, no club listing — **without deleting it**, on a guardian's or a club's request. **And it has been done once, to a test record, by a person.** | ☐ |
| **A2** | **A documented path exists to remove one guardian's access**, and it works the way §4.10 requires: **suppress first, point to 1800RESPECT, permanent removal only on a court order.** *Nobody here adjudicates a family dispute.* | ☐ |
| **A3** | **A declared date of birth cannot be amended upward** without guardian or club confirmation. | ☐ |
| **A4** | **The age-contradiction hold runs** — an account claiming to be 19 while naming a U15 squad is held, not published. | ☐ |
| **A5** | **A report route exists for "this account belongs to a child"** that needs neither an account nor certainty, and it has been used once end to end. | ☐ |
| **A6** | **Every under-18 page carries `noindex, nofollow` and is excluded from the sitemap** — *and this has been verified on the deployed site, not in the source.* **D-18 promises no search. Google is a search.** | ☐ |
| **A7** | **No third-party video, image or embed loads until a viewer presses play.** Nothing reaches YouTube, Instagram or Veo from a page whose subject is a minor until a human acts. | ☐ |

> **A6 is the one I would check last and personally.** It is a header and a robots directive — the cheapest row on this page and the only one whose failure is silently permanent, because a page indexed once is in a cache we do not control.

# D · Named, and explicitly NOT blocking

*Recorded here so they are not argued about on the morning, and so nobody mistakes a real risk for a gate.*

**The doc 31 rulings are not numbered.** Twelve entries are written out and waiting in the Board Room. **This blocks the build, not the launch** — the documents already say the right thing. But three of them describe behaviour that does not exist yet (the 24-hour undo, the abuse counter, the child-safety reason class), and **B6 applies to them exactly as it applies to 6.5: a document must not promise what is not built.** *If they are still unnumbered on the morning, the honest fix is to hold those sentences back, not to ship them as promises.*

**Doc 28 is unsigned.** The company does not own the IP it sells access to. **That is a raise-and-sale problem, not a launch problem**, and I am not going to inflate it into one. It takes ten minutes and it should be done anyway.

**There is no second responder.** Docs 22, 24 and 25 publish a five-business-day appeal and an urgent route, all resting on one person. **That is honest today only because all three documents say so in the open.** I have now raised it four times. **It should stop being a recommendation and become a risk BUZ accepts by D-number**, so it is on the record as a choice rather than an oversight — and so I stop repeating it.

**No solicitor has been engaged and the D-27 gate is unmet.** Recorded once, on BUZ's direction of 27 August. Not re-raised here.
