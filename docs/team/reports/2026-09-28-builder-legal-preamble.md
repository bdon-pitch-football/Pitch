Lesson: **a proxy check can survive into the commit that makes it wrong, and then it argues for the bug.** `leg16` first asserted "the consent stamp hashes the source file" — true, and a fair proxy for "a row resolves to the text that person read" while the file and the page were the same bytes. My own change made them different bytes, and the check went on passing while now standing for the opposite of its rule: it would have held a guardian's row against 713 words she never saw. The safety seat caught it, not me, and the tell was in my own report — I wrote down that the source and the served bytes now differ, and did not go back and ask what else in the codebase had been relying on their being the same. When a change makes two things stop being identical, grep for everything that compares, hashes or copies one into the other (L33, L25).
