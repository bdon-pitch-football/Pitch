## Screen 1 · The parent approval screen — `/a/[id]`, `/g/pending/[recordId]`
*Every parent who approves a child meets these two lines.*

**1.** `app/a/[id]/page.tsx:34` — the under-16 version of the four promises
**Current:** "No one can contact them directly." / "Every approach comes to you together."
**Proposed:** "No one can contact them directly." / "Any approach from outside their club comes to you both together."
**Rule:** consistency. Doc 15 §2's build note requires the four promises in near-identical words across the screen and the email.
**Why:** the 16–17 version of this exact promise, eight lines up the same file, already reads that way — and "every approach" reads as a bigger claim than we make, since a coach at their own club can speak to them at training.

**2.** `app/a/[id]/page.tsx:35` and `app/g/pending/[recordId]/page.tsx:52`
**Current (`a/[id]`):** "You hold the share link." / "It works only where you send it, expires every 90 days, and you can pause or regenerate it any time."
**Current (`g/pending`):** "Expires in 90 days. You can pause or regenerate it any time."
**Proposed (both):** "…pause or replace it at any time."
**Rule:** consistency, and plain words a parent reads once.
**Why:** doc 15 §2 and the button on the controls screen both say "replace"; "regenerate" is our word, not a parent's.

## Screen 2 · Messages that send — `lib/messages.ts` and doc 15
*Rule 5 of the training pack: if a message is not in doc 15 word for word, it does not send.
Each of these either goes into doc 15 with your yes, or the code reverts to doc 15.
Line numbers are as at commit `e2d85ac`; `lib/messages.ts` is being edited today, so the doc 15
section number identifies the text, not the line.*

**3.** §20 — a send waiting on the guardian of an under-16 (`lib/messages.ts:201`)
**Current (sends):** "Check the address and send: pitchfootball.com.au/g/send/{id}" and nothing else.
**Doc 15 §20 has:** "**[Check the address and send]** · **[Not this one]**"
**Proposed:** approve the email as it sends, and change doc 15 §20 to drop **[Not this one]**.
**Rule:** rule 5.
**Why:** doing nothing already is the "not this one" — the next paragraph says so — and a button that only repeats silence invites a parent to think a decision is owed. Harmless either way, but the doc and the email should not differ.

**4.** §22 — your child sent their CV, to the guardian of a 16–17 (`lib/messages.ts:253`)
**Current (sends):** subject "{Name} sent their CV to {club}"; "{Name} does not need your approval for this — at sixteen and seventeen, sending is theirs to do."; "See what they sent, or turn sending off: pitchfootball.com.au/g/controls/{childId}"
**Doc 15 §22 has:** "Nate sent **his** CV…", "sending is **his** to do", and two buttons, "**[See what he sent]** · **[Turn sending off]**"
**Proposed:** approve the sending text into doc 15 §22 word for word.
**Rule:** rule 5, and data minimisation — the product holds no gender for a child, so it cannot write "his".
**Why:** the code is right on both counts; doc 15's example reads as if we know something about the child that we deliberately never collect.

**5.** §19 — a CV sent to a club, the opening line when an adult or a 16–17 sends it themselves (`lib/messages.ts:161`)
**Current (sends):** "{Name} has sent you their football CV."
**Doc 15 §19 has only:** "Deniz's family has sent you Deniz's football CV."
**Proposed:** approve the self-sent opener into doc 15 §19 as a second variant.
**Rule:** rule 5.
**Why:** doc 15 was written before anyone could send their own CV; saying "their family sent this" about a nineteen-year-old is wrong in the club's inbox.

**6.** §19 — the control paragraph, adult version
**Current (sends):** "This is a link, not a file. {Name} controls it — they can switch it off or replace it at any time, and it expires on its own. If it stops working, that is their choice, not a fault."
**Proposed:** approve into doc 15 §19 as the 18+ variant.
**Rule:** rule 5.
**Why:** doc 15 only has the family version, which tells a club an adult's page is controlled by their parents.

**7.** §19 — the control paragraph, 16–17 self-sent version
**Current (sends):** "This is a link, not a file. {Name} and their family control it — they can pause or replace it at any time, and it expires on its own. If it stops working, that is normal and it is their choice, not a fault."
**Proposed:** approve into doc 15 §19 as the 16–17 variant.
**Rule:** rule 5.
**Why:** it is the accurate one for that band — the player sends, the parent still holds the switch.

**8.** §19 — the reply route, adult version
**Current (sends):** "Replies to this message do not reach {Name}. Pitch does not pass messages on."
**Proposed:** approve into doc 15 §19 as the 18+ variant.
**Rule:** rule 5.
**Why:** doc 15's version is written about a child and a family; an adult's CV needs the same fact said without the safeguarding frame.

**9.** §19 — the trial paragraph, under-18 version
**Current (sends):** "If you want {Name} at a trial, post it on Pitch. Families register their interest from your trial, and that is where you can invite them — it goes to {Name} and their parent together, and a record is kept."
**Doc 15 §19 has:** "If you want Deniz at a trial, post it on Pitch **or send an invitation through their guardian**. Both go to the parent, and both keep a record."
**Proposed:** approve the sending text into doc 15 §19.
**Rule:** rule 5, and never name a route we do not offer.
**Why:** the product has one route — the register on a trial notice — and doc 15 offers a club two, one of which it cannot find.

**10.** §19 — the footer, when the player sent it themselves
**Current (sends):** "You received this because a player sent you their CV. We did not add you to a list and there is nothing to unsubscribe from."
**Proposed:** approve into doc 15 §19 as the self-sent variant.
**Rule:** rule 5.
**Why:** doc 15 says "a family sent you their child's CV", which is the wrong sentence on an adult's send and the line that stops a club marking us as spam.

**11.** §5 and §23 — the share-link renewal reminder (`lib/messages.ts:646`, `:665`)
**Current (sends):** "Renew for another 90 days: pitchfootball.com.au/g/controls/{childId}" then, on the next line, "Let it expire: there's nothing to do."
**Doc 15 §5 has:** "**[Renew for another 90 days]** · **[Let it expire]**", with the note that letting it expire is a real button, weighted equally.
**Proposed:** approve the two lines as they send, and amend doc 15 §5 and §23 to match — keeping the note, because the two options still sit on consecutive lines with the same weight.
**Rule:** rule 5, and D-53 — expiry is a good choice and must never be quietly discouraged.
**Why:** a plain-text email has no buttons, and "there's nothing to do" is the truest version of the equal weighting: the choice we would rather they felt free to make is the one that costs no tap. **This changes approved doc 15 text, so it needs your yes rather than Leo's.**

**12.** §31 — the club's receipt (`lib/messages.ts:429`)
**Current (sends):** "Cancel within 14 days of today and we refund the whole {amount}, no questions." — **only on the annual plan.**
**Doc 15 §31 has:** the same sentence with no condition.
**Proposed:** approve the condition into doc 15 §31 by marking the sentence "annual plan only".
**Rule:** rule 5, and D-136 (the cooling-off is on the annual plan).
**Why:** the code follows D-136 and the doc promises every monthly club a refund it would not get — and a receipt is exactly where a treasurer reads the promise back to us.
