The shells, Floodlit

One idea: Floodlit goes into the parts, not the pages. The seat bar, the rail, the page header, the panel, the pill, the row, the hero, the empty tile, the table, the quiet shell and the failure shell change once, and all 74 pages move with them. The exact CSS, the class names and the part names are in docs/design/specs/A-shells-and-homes.md (section “Shell parts”). Every other group refers to these parts by name.

The five things a person will notice. (1) From 1024px the logo sits top left, at the head of the rail (D-173 (3)); on a phone it stays top right. (2) “Where you are” is ink, not green, in the bar, the rail and the More sheet: green is an action (D-173 (4)). (3) Every panel gets the card shadow from a token, and the lifted one gets the floating shadow (D-173 (1)). (4) The bar, the rail and the nav bar are one glass. (5) Pages with no frame get the top bar, so the logo rule holds on them too.

Fixed and kept: every door in every frame and the ≤4 rule for the bar (three doors plus More, with Sign out always in More); the 1024 rail and 768 table breakpoints; the charter’s type scale, letter-spacings, radii, two buttons and 44px targets; and every word.
