The parent screens (/a, /g/*, /join/waiting, /privacy/family)

One idea: every question a parent is asked is one answer sheet. It reads top to bottom as who is asking (the child’s tile and a purple kicker: a state, waiting on you) → the question → what happens if yes (one card of tick rows) → what happens if you don’t (the sunken do-nothing line) → two equal answers. From 640 the sheet sits on the approved door panel; on a phone it is the column as drawn. A list (the controls, Your family) is a page, not a panel.

The consent-button decision. Where a press gives something away — makes a child’s page live, sends a CV, puts a child on a register, publishes a change, approves a card, replies to a club, puts a child in a squad — Yes and No are the same charter secondary button, the same size, side by side from 1024 (.fl-answer). Nothing on a consent answer glows. Today every one of these uses the glowing green primary for Yes, and No is either a plain div that does nothing (four screens), an 11.5px muted footnote (the approval), or a narrow ghost (the squad invitation). A step that gives nothing away (“Yes, it’s me — continue”, “Email me the link”, “Sign in”, “Get the share link”) keeps the one glowing primary.

Fixed and kept: every word is verbatim from the source; order on the phone; every route, form field and destination (the ones that would move are PD-1 and PD-2); what a parent sees about a child and what a club sees; D-126, D-82, D-163, D-172. No type size changes and no new width. Seed names only (Mila and Priya Raman, Alex with Deniz, Nate and Georgia, Riverside FC, Quarrymead United, Kingsway Rovers FC). Nate and Jordan stand in where the seed has no row for a state; the report ID and the replaced link token are invented.

green works under the rules as they stand. amber needs a decision from BUZ (numbered as in docs/design/specs/D-parent.md). red is wrong today.
