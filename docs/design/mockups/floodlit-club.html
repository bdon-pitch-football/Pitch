The club console (/club/*)

One idea: the back office is built from the shop floor’s parts. A technical director works the register on a laptop and then opens the club page, the trials board and the jobs board that families and coaches read. Every console screen here uses the parts those public screens already use: the Floodlit card and its two shadows, the trial row (.tr, from /fc and /trials), the role row (from /jobs), and the club hero (from /fc/[slug]). A list is a page: one Floodlit card of hairline rows, which becomes the table from 768. A form is a door: one panel. A create form above a list folds behind one chip (the trials board’s disclosure), and it opens by itself when the list is empty. Nothing is invented, and there’s no new token or colour.

Colour, D-173 (4): the register keeps its three status colours (New green, Shortlisted amber, Invited purple) on its numerals and its status pills, as group A’s stat rule says (A-shells-and-homes.md part 15). Everything else that was coloured without being an action or a state goes neutral: the first-choice position chip in a squad sheet, the Paid tag and the amber club name above the invite. A club’s own colours show only in the page editor’s preview of its own club page, and never on an unclaimed club (D-172).

Fixed and kept: every word (verbatim from app/club/**/page.tsx, components/RegisterPaused.tsx and components/cv/PlayerCV.tsx), the 390 order, every door, field and button destination, and every read. Seed only: Riverside FC (100 registrations, Marina Petrovic TD, Robyn Callister administrator), Kingsway Rovers FC (verified, one registration via its own trial), Quarrymead United (claimed and unverified, 4 held) and the house players Deniz, Nate, Georgia and Jordan, from scripts/dev-db.mts and lib/fixtures.ts. Registration counts are the seed’s shape, not a promise. The dashed italic boxes are notes on the board, not product.

The console frame (seat bar below 1024, the rail with the rail mark and seat card from 1024, the page header, the panel, the pill, the door) is group A’s (docs/design/specs/A-shells-and-homes.md). It’s drawn here in outline only. F designs what sits inside it.

green works under the rules as they stand. amber needs a decision from BUZ (P1–P6 in docs/design/specs/F-club.md). red is what the source does today, drawn so the difference is visible. Amber-underlined words or an amber-dotted outline mark a reused line in a new place, or new content, for BUZ.
