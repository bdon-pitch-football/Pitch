Sign-up, sign-in and the claim (/join, /signin, /claim, /claim/[slug])

One idea: a form is a door, a list is a page. Every step that asks for something sits in one Floodlit panel on the floodlight, with one glowing primary; every page that shows a list (Find your club) sits on the page itself. On a phone there is no panel at all: the 390 artboard is the column as drawn, and from 640 the same column is lifted onto the panel. That is a container change and nothing else (D-147).

The same parts as the built Floodlit pages: the nav bar (logo right on a phone, left from 1024), the light search field from /, .fl-card and --shadow-float, the club page’s dashed tile for a club that is not claimed yet, and the one field well (.field) on every form, where today sign-in, sign-up and the claim each use a different one.

Fixed and kept: every word verbatim from the five files; the fields and their order; the country asked first and the overseas path collecting nothing (D-63, D-164(3)); nothing on an unclaimed club that is an image or says it is with us (D-172); no price anywhere (D-163). The only new words are in the report’s copy list. Seed names only (Jordan, Nate, Dana Kovac, Brindlewood Rovers SC, Kingsway Rovers FC, Westgate Rangers). “Glenmarsh United” is invented for the no-result search.

green works under the rules as they stand. amber needs a decision from BUZ (numbered as in docs/design/reports/2026-10-01-proposal-trials-and-join.md). red is something wrong today.
