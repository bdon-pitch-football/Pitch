Every seat’s home, Floodlit

The one idea: every home is the same three layers. First, who and where you are, in the hero panel. Second, the one thing to do next: the lead notice, or the screen’s single glowing primary. Third, everything else, as quiet rows: the grey “menu cards” that repeated the sidebar become one door list, with the same doors and the same words. BUZ’s test is that a stranger can tell in five seconds what this screen is for and what to do next. The hero answers the first half, and the one glow answers the second.

Built only from the shell parts in docs/design/specs/A-shells-and-homes.md, referred to by name. Nothing on these screens is new data. Every word is verbatim from app/home/page.tsx and the components it renders (WhileYouWereAway, SquadCard, RegisterReaders, RegisterPaused, CopyLink). People, clubs and trials are the seed’s (scripts/dev-db.mts, lib/fixtures.ts). Register counts follow the seed’s shape (4 fixtures plus 96 generated rows at Riverside).

Fixed and kept: the order on the 390 artboard, every door, every condition (who sees what: D-93, D-126, D-154, D-82), no zero ever drawn as a number (D-162), and no price (D-163: the administrator’s Plan block is not drawn, because billing is off).

no decision means it works under the rules as they stand. decision needs BUZ; the changed element has a dashed amber outline. new line means a line for BUZ’s approval (dashed purple outline), or a line that is untrue today.
