The player's screens: the CV builder, Send my CV, the share card, register interest, where you play, /manage and the print CV

One idea: everything a player makes is the player card. The public CV is now a card (approved, components/cv/PlayerCV.tsx). The builder is where that card gets made, so its first step draws the card-making fields on the card: the photo, the name, the positions, the number and the foot, on the card's own gradient, with the squad number standing behind them as it does on the CV. The preview shows the card itself under one header. "Your page is ready" shows the card's ticket. The share card's three shapes are drawn as card silhouettes, never rendered from the record. The print CV is the card in ink. Everything else a player does is a question or a list, so it follows the approved house rule: a form is a door, a list is a page.

The parts are spec A's (specs/A-shells-and-homes.md): Frame, Seat bar, Rail with the rail mark, Top bar, Page header, Page title, Section heading, Panel heading, Panel, Well, Notice, Pill, List row, Empty tile, Buttons with the one glow, Field, Door panel and Quiet shell. The parts this group adds are listed in the spec under "New shared parts": --cv-hero-bg, .cv-edit, .checks, .pos-grid, .notice-h, .pv-strip and the print tokens.
