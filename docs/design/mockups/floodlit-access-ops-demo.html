I · desk density, Floodlit manners. The console is built from A’s parts only: rail with the mark and seat card, the page header, the 17px operator title, the table as one lifted card, a form as one panel, the pill for every state, the empty tile for every empty list, the well for every standing rule. J · the real product, clearly marked. /demo is drawn from the sign-up choice card; after a seat is taken the club console is the real one, with the demo strip moved off green (green is an action) onto the amber state pill.

green works under the rules as they stand. amber needs BUZ (numbered as in the three specs). red is wrong today. Seed names only; anything illustrative says so. Same markup in both frames: the frames are containers and the breakpoints are container queries.
