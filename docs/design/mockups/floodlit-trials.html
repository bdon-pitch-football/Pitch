The trials board (/trials)

One idea: the board is built from the club page’s parts. The trial row here is the trial row on /fc/[slug] (date first, the same numeral, the same stamps and the same “The club’s own notice”), in the same Floodlit card, under the same nav bar. A family who goes from the board to a club sees one object, not two. Nothing is invented: no new component, no new token, no new colour.

Fixed and kept: every word (verbatim from app/trials/page.tsx), the fields on each listing, the 390 order (title, filters, what is chosen and the count, the how-it-works note, the listings), chronological order with no recommender, the four filters, every listing’s “checked” date and the club’s own notice link (D-90, John 30 Sep), no image on an unclaimed club’s listing (D-172). Seed content only: Riverside FC, Westgate Rangers and Kingsway Rovers FC are fixtures from scripts/dev-db.mts.

Two decisions run through every artboard: the nav bar on the signed-out board is P2, and every 1280 artboard shows the filters as a rail beside the list, which is P1. Without P1 the same rows and cards sit in the 640 column with the filter card above them, as today; without P2 the bar is the logo alone.

green works under the rules as they stand. amber needs a decision from BUZ, numbered as in the proposal report (docs/design/reports/2026-10-01-proposal-trials-and-join.md).
