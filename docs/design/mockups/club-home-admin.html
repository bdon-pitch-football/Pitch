A club administrator's /home

63% empty at 1280, and it is the worst first-run impression of any signed-in seat — a club administrator is very often the first person at a club to open Pitch.

What is wrong now

Two separate failures, both caused by designing one screen and rendering it for two people. The hero card was built for the technical director and carries her 100 / 80 / 10 / 10 register row. An administrator has no register access, which is correct (D-93, D-154) — so for Robyn the same card renders three lines of text and about 50px of blank, and reads as a card that failed to load. And the right rail is six identical secondary buttons with no primary: Post a trial, Squads, Crest & club page, Coaching roles, Your club page, Plan & billing — which is the sidebar immediately to their left, word for word. There is no accent anywhere on the screen, so the eye has nowhere to land.

What the change does for the person on this screen

It answers the administrator's actual question, which is not "where can I go" (the sidebar already answers that) but "is our club set up properly, and what is a family seeing right now?" The hero carries the three numbers she is entitled to — squads, live trial notices, open coaching roles — none of which touches a registration. The rail stops repeating the sidebar and holds the club's public state: the page's own URL, when it was last changed, what a family cannot see yet, and who at this club can do what. And the screen gets exactly one primary.

The last block is the one I would fight for. "Who can do what here" is the only place in the product where the D-93 role split is said out loud to the person it constrains. Robyn finding out she cannot read the register by clicking something and getting nothing is the version we ship today.

What it costs to build

Low. Squad count, trial notices and open roles are already queried on this page for the technical director's variant. The club page's "last changed" is a timestamp on the club row. The "who can do what" list is the same register_grant query /club/squads already runs for the TD, plus the membership rows. No new permission, no new table.

What it does NOT change

Not what an administrator may see. No registration count, no child's name, no held-registration detail reaches this screen — the numbers on it are squads, notices and roles, which are the club's own furniture. Not the technical director's version of this page, which keeps its register row and is not in this proposal. Not the sidebar. Not the phone: at 390 the rail's blocks stack under the main column in the artboard's order.

The unverified first-run variant (the club has claimed its page but nobody has rung them yet) is not drawn here. It keeps the amber "Awaiting verification — registrations are held" line exactly as built, and the same three blocks below it; the register numerals are simply absent, which is what D-126 requires.
